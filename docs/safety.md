# Safety and data protection

- Use fictional or authorized de-identified inputs for training.
- Approvals must be enforced by your application or workflow, not merely requested in a prompt.
- Limit connector permissions and avoid sharing credentials in prompts.
- Do not let a support agent authorize refunds, payments, or personal-data exports.
- Assess GDPR and sector-specific responsibilities before using real records.
- Stop and review the workflow after an unexpected action or sensitive-data incident.
